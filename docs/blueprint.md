# **App Name**: NomadGuide AI

## Core Features:

- AI-Powered Narrative Tours: Generate real-time, context-aware audio narration for points of interest using a generative AI tool, adapting storytelling style based on user preferences and location.
- Interactive Map Navigation: Integrate Google Maps for real-time turn-by-turn navigation, dynamically displaying upcoming POIs and route information tailored to the user's journey.
- Personalized POI Discovery: Utilize an AI tool to recommend relevant points of interest along the planned route based on user interests, historical interactions, and real-time contextual factors.
- Immersive 3D Landmark Previews: Display interactive 3D GLTF models of upcoming or selected landmarks, synchronized with audio cues and animated camera movements for an enriched preview experience.
- Seamless Authentication & Preferences: Enable frictionless login (email+OTP or SSO) and provide a user profile interface to manage interests, preferred narration styles, and audio mix settings.
- Adaptive Audio Experience: Orchestrate a dynamic audio soundscape, seamlessly transitioning between AI narration, contextual sound effects, and adaptive background music during the tour.
- Offline Asset Caching: Allow users to pre-download and cache POI narration assets, 3D GLTF bundles, and navigation data for reliable offline tour functionality.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A rich, deep blue-violet (#6E2BCC) evoking intellect, curiosity, and wonder, ideal for representing technology and exploration.
- Background color: A muted, deep slate (#1C1B1E), providing a high-contrast and immersive backdrop that reduces eye strain during navigation.
- Accent color: A bright, clear sky blue (#8BB8FF), used for interactive elements, highlights, and calls to action, drawing attention without being distracting.
- Headlines: 'Space Grotesk' (sans-serif) for a modern, tech-savvy aesthetic and strong visual presence.
- Body text: 'Inter' (sans-serif) chosen for its high readability and neutrality, suitable for long-form narration and detailed descriptions.
- Use clean, contemporary line icons with subtle rounding, ensuring clarity and quick recognition on a dark themed background, aligned with digital innovation.
- Adopt a minimalist, card-based UI focusing on glanceability and essential contextual information during navigation, optimized for mobile interaction while driving or on the go.
- Implement smooth, subtle transitions for map interactions and screen changes. Feature fluid animations for 3D model loading and camera adjustments to enhance user engagement and visual feedback.
